iOS top edge effect: reproduction pages

On an iPhone running iOS 27, open a page in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from the Home Screen and look at the fixed toolbar against the red ruler.

  • Bug: black-translucent; the toolbar is transparent over the status bar.
  • Opaque toolbar: black-translucent; the toolbar has a plain white background from the top of the screen.
  • Control: default.

The report, the source of these pages and the test results are at github.com/benark/ios-webview-edge-effect-test.
